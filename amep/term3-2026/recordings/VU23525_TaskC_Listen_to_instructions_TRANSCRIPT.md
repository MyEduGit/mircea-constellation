## Official transcript (verbatim, spoken content)
In this video lesson, I’m going to show you how to lift a box. The techniques I’ll demonstrate
are not just for boxes. Knowing how to lift properly is useful for many situations in life and
at work. For example, stacking shelves in a supermarket, delivering parcels, picking up shopping
bags or small children. If you know how to lift correctly, you can protect your back and avoid
injury. So, let’s get started… Step 1 – First check the box. Before you lift something, test to
see how heavy it is. You can do this by pushing the box with your feet or hands. If it doesn't
move easily, it's pretty heavy. You should ask someone for help. Step 2 – Position your body
correctly. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, look straight ahead, and keep your back
straight. Step 3 – Hold the box. Keep your back straight and kneel down in front of the box.
Grip the box with two hands. Step 4 – Lastly, slowly lift the box. Use your leg muscles to lift
– not your back. Push your body up to a standing position. When you are carrying the box, hold
it close to your body and don’t twist your back. Walk slowly so you don’t trip over. When you
have finished, keep your back straight as you put the box back down. That’s it – I hope you
found this useful.
