## Official transcript (verbatim, spoken content)
Hello, my name is Doctor Sami, and I am going to talk to you today about eating for good health.
Eating healthy food will decrease a number of health associated illnesses. Eating healthy food
lowers the risk of developing health problems, such as heart disease, obesity and type 2
diabetes. Eating healthy can also make you feel good and helps you with your energy levels.
Choosing the right food choices can make a really big difference to your diet, health and
wellbeing. Eating healthy and nutritious foods is one way you can lower the risk of developing
many chronic health problems such as diabetes heart disease and obesity. Having a healthy diet
can help in managing health conditions, you may also feel better, look better, enjoy life more
and live longer. Eating the wrong foods, can make you unhealthy and become unwell which could
lead you to putting on weight, developing high cholesterol and the risk of getting high blood
pressure. These can lead to heart disease, stroke, diabetes, some cancers and other chronic
illnesses. You don’t necessarily need to make big changes to get positive benefits. Small
changes to your eating habits and adding more physical activity will help you feel healthier,
manage your health conditions and lower the risk of many chronic diseases. By eating the
recommended amounts from the five food groups and limiting the foods that are high in saturated
fat, added sugars and added salt, are the essentials for good health. Start to think about what
you can do to improve in your lifestyle and health. It isn’t that hard to decide to eat well.
