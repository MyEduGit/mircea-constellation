# AI Literacy for ESL Students
## A Practical Guide for Adult Migrant English Learners

**Author:** Mircea Matthews
**Target:** Amazon KDP (eBook + Paperback) + Gumroad (digital)
**Price point:** $9.99 eBook / $14.99 paperback
**Audience:** ESL teachers, AMEP programs, adult migrant learners (Australia + global)
**Unique angle:** Written by an actual AMEP teacher with 21 active students

## Revenue Model
- Amazon KDP: 70% royalty on eBook ($7/sale), ~60% on paperback
- Gumroad: 90%+ after fees
- Teachers Pay Teachers: supplementary worksheets ($3-5 each)
- Bulk licensing to AMEP providers nationally

## Structure (10 chapters, ~25,000 words)

### Part 1: Foundation
1. What is AI? (Plain English, real examples)
2. AI Tools You Can Use Today (ChatGPT, Google Translate, Grammarly)
3. Staying Safe Online with AI

### Part 2: AI for English Learning
4. Using AI to Practice Speaking
5. Using AI to Practice Writing
6. Using AI to Build Vocabulary
7. AI for Reading Comprehension

### Part 3: AI for Daily Life in Australia
8. AI for Job Searching and Resumes
9. AI for Government Services and Forms
10. AI for Healthcare and Community

### Appendices
- Glossary of AI terms (simple English)
- QR codes linking to free AI tools
- Worksheet templates (photocopiable)
- Teacher's guide with lesson plans

## Competitive Advantage
- No book like this exists for ESL/AMEP specifically
- Written from real classroom experience (Cert I, II, III levels)
- Includes Australian context (Centrelink, Medicare, myGov)
- Multi-level: each chapter has Cert I (beginner), II, III versions
- Worksheet pack sold separately = additional revenue stream
