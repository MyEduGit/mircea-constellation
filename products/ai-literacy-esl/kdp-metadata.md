## Description (4,000 char max for KDP)
Are you learning English as a second language? Do you feel overwhelmed by technology, government forms, and job applications in a new country?

This book is for you.

AI Literacy for ESL Students is the first practical guide written specifically for adult migrants learning English. Written by an experienced AMEP (Adult Migrant English Program) teacher in Melbourne, Australia, this book shows you how to use free AI tools like ChatGPT, Google Translate, Grammarly, and ELSA Speak to:

- Practice speaking English without fear of judgement
- Write emails, resumes, and cover letters
- Understand government letters from Centrelink and Medicare
- Prepare for job interviews
- Build vocabulary for real-life situations (shopping, doctors, school)
- Stay safe from online scams
- Navigate Australian government services
- Find community support and healthcare

Every chapter includes:
- Step-by-step instructions with screenshots
- Three difficulty levels (beginner, intermediate, advanced)
- Practice activities you can do at home
- Teacher's notes for classroom use
- Key vocabulary tables

Whether you are a student learning English, a teacher looking for modern resources, or a community worker helping migrants settle, this book gives you practical tools you can use today.

No technical knowledge required. Just a phone and the willingness to learn.
