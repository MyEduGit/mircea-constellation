# Chapter 4: Using AI to Practice Speaking

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this chapter, you will:
- Use AI to practise speaking English at home
- Know how to have a conversation with ChatGPT using voice
- Improve your pronunciation with ELSA Speak

---

## Why Practice Speaking with AI?

Many ESL students say: *"I understand English, but I am scared to speak."*

AI can help because:
- **AI does not judge you** — it will never laugh at your mistakes
- **AI is always available** — practise at midnight if you want
- **AI is patient** — it will repeat the same thing 100 times
- **AI is free** — no cost for speaking practice

---

## Method 1: Voice Chat with ChatGPT

The ChatGPT app (iPhone and Android) has a **voice mode**.

**How to use it:**
1. Open the ChatGPT app
2. Tap the headphone icon at the bottom
3. Speak in English (or your language)
4. ChatGPT will listen and respond with voice

**Great prompts to try:**

*"I am an ESL student at Cert II level. Please have a simple conversation with me about my weekend. Correct my mistakes gently."*

*"I have a job interview tomorrow for a cleaning job. Please practise interview questions with me. Speak slowly."*

*"Let's practise ordering food at a restaurant. You are the waiter. I am the customer."*

**Tip:** Tell ChatGPT your level. Say *"I am a beginner"* or *"I am intermediate."* It will adjust.

## Method 2: ELSA Speak for Pronunciation

ELSA Speak listens to your pronunciation and scores you.

**How it works:**
1. ELSA shows you a sentence
2. You read it out loud
3. ELSA highlights which sounds were good (green) and which need work (red)
4. You try again

**Focus areas for ESL students:**
- The "th" sound (this, that, think)
- The "v" vs "w" sound (very vs. wery)
- Word stress (DEsert vs. deSERT)
- The "r" and "l" sounds

---

## Method 3: Record Yourself

This method uses no special app — just your phone's voice recorder.

1. Choose a topic (e.g., "My family")
2. Record yourself speaking for 1 minute
3. Listen to your recording
4. Type what you said into ChatGPT
5. Ask: *"Please correct my English and explain the mistakes"*
6. Record yourself again with the corrections

This is how professional language learners improve. You are training your ear and your mouth at the same time.

## Daily Speaking Practice Plan

| Day | Activity | Time |
|-----|---------|------|
| Monday | Voice chat with ChatGPT (free topic) | 10 min |
| Tuesday | ELSA Speak pronunciation drills | 10 min |
| Wednesday | Record yourself, correct with ChatGPT | 15 min |
| Thursday | Voice chat: practise a real scenario (shop, doctor, bank) | 10 min |
| Friday | ELSA Speak + record improvement | 15 min |
| Weekend | Watch an English YouTube video, repeat 3 sentences out loud | 10 min |

**Total: about 10-15 minutes per day. That is all you need.**

## Practice Activities

### Activity 1: First Voice Chat (Cert I)
Open ChatGPT voice mode. Say: *"Hello, my name is [your name]. I am learning English."*
Listen to ChatGPT's response. Did you understand it? YES / NO

### Activity 2: Role Play (Cert II)
Use ChatGPT voice mode for this scenario:
- You are at the doctor
- Tell the doctor you have a headache and a sore throat
- Ask for a medical certificate for work

Write down two new words you learned: _______________, _______________

### Activity 3: Before and After (Cert III)
1. Record yourself talking about your daily routine (1 minute)
2. Get corrections from ChatGPT
3. Record yourself again with the corrections
4. Listen to both recordings

What improved? Write 2-3 sentences:
_______________________________________________

## Teacher's Note
Voice chat with ChatGPT is a game-changer for students who won't speak in class. Assign it as homework — students practise privately at home and build confidence before speaking in front of classmates.

ELSA Speak's free tier is limited but sufficient for classroom use. The paid version ($4/month) is worth recommending to motivated students.

Estimated class time: 60 minutes (including setup)
Materials needed: students' phones, earphones recommended
