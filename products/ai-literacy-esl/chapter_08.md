## Writing a Resume with AI

Many students say: *"I don't know how to write an Australian resume."*

AI can write one for you in 5 minutes.

### Step-by-step:

**Open ChatGPT and type:**

*"I need an Australian-style resume. Here is my information:*
*Name: [your name]*
*Phone: [your phone]*
*Email: [your email]*
*I worked as a [your job] in [your country] for [how many years].*
*I am studying English at AMEP in Melbourne.*
*I want to find work as a [cleaner / kitchen hand / warehouse worker / etc].*
*Please write a professional resume."*

ChatGPT will create a full resume. Then:
1. Copy it into a Word document or Google Doc
2. Read it carefully — make sure all the facts are correct
3. Ask your teacher to check it
4. Save it as a PDF

**Important:** AI may add skills you don't have. Always check and remove anything that is not true.

## Preparing for Job Interviews

### Practice with ChatGPT:

*"I have a job interview for a kitchen hand position at a restaurant. Please ask me common interview questions one at a time. After I answer, tell me how to improve my answer."*

### Common interview questions AI will practise with you:
1. "Tell me about yourself."
2. "Why do you want this job?"
3. "What experience do you have?"
4. "When can you start?"
5. "Can you work weekends?"

### The STAR method (for Cert III students):
Ask ChatGPT: *"Teach me the STAR method for answering interview questions with an example."*

STAR = **S**ituation, **T**ask, **A**ction, **R**esult

## Finding Jobs with AI

| Tool | What it does | Cost |
|------|-------------|------|
| **Seek.com.au** | Australia's main job website — uses AI to match you with jobs | Free |
| **Indeed.com.au** | Global job search — AI-powered recommendations | Free |
| **LinkedIn** | Professional network — AI suggests jobs and connections | Free |
| **Google Jobs** | Search "jobs near me" on Google — AI collects from all sites | Free |
| **ChatGPT** | Ask: "What entry-level jobs can I get in Melbourne with basic English?" | Free |

### AI Job Search Tip:
Type into ChatGPT: *"I live in [your suburb]. I speak [your language] and basic English. I have no Australian work experience. What jobs should I apply for? Give me a list with the company type and how to apply."*

## Writing a Cover Letter with AI

A cover letter is a short letter you send with your resume.

**Type into ChatGPT:**

*"Write a cover letter for a cleaning job at [company name]. I am a new migrant, I am learning English at AMEP, I am reliable and hardworking. Keep it simple and professional."*

Then:
1. Read it — does it sound like you?
2. Change anything that is not true
3. Add your name and phone number
4. Send it with your resume

## Key Words

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Resume (CV) | A document listing your work experience and skills |
| Cover letter | A short letter explaining why you want the job |
| Interview | A meeting where the employer asks you questions |
| Entry-level | A job that does not need experience |
| Reliable | Someone you can depend on — they always come to work |
| Professional | Formal and suitable for work |

## Practice Activities

### Activity 1: Build Your Resume (Cert I)
Use ChatGPT to create your resume. Print it. Bring it to class.

### Activity 2: Mock Interview (Cert II)
Use ChatGPT voice mode to practise three interview questions. Write down:
- The question ChatGPT asked: _______________
- Your answer: _______________
- How ChatGPT said you could improve: _______________

### Activity 3: Complete Job Application (Cert III)
1. Find a real job on seek.com.au that you could do
2. Use ChatGPT to help write your cover letter
3. Have your teacher review it
4. Apply for the job (if appropriate)

Job title: _______________
Company: _______________
Did you apply? YES / NO
