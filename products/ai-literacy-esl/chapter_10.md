## At the Doctor

### Before Your Appointment

Ask ChatGPT:

*"I am going to the doctor. I have [describe your problem]. How do I explain this in English? Give me the exact words to say."*

**Example:**
*"I have a pain in my lower back. It started two weeks ago. It is worse in the morning. I took Panadol but it did not help. Give me the English sentences to tell my doctor."*

ChatGPT will give you ready-to-use sentences. You can read them from your phone at the appointment.

### Understanding Your Doctor

After the appointment, if you didn't understand something:

*"My doctor said I have 'gastroesophageal reflux.' What is this in simple English? What should I do?"*

Simple answer: *"This means stomach acid goes up into your throat. It causes heartburn. You should avoid spicy food, eat smaller meals, and take the medicine your doctor gave you."*

### Medicine Labels

Take a photo of your medicine bottle or box. Upload to ChatGPT:

*"Please explain this medicine label in simple English. How many should I take? When? Are there any warnings?"*

## Mental Health

Moving to a new country is stressful. It is normal to feel:
- Lonely
- Worried
- Homesick
- Overwhelmed

AI can help you find support:

*"I am a new migrant in [your city]. I feel very stressed and lonely. What free mental health services are available for me?"*

**Free services in Australia:**
- **Beyond Blue:** 1300 22 4636 (24/7)
- **Lifeline:** 13 11 14 (24/7)
- **TIS (interpreter):** 131 450 (free interpreter for any phone call)

**You can call TIS first.** They will call the health service for you and translate in real time. This service is free.

## Finding Community Services

Ask ChatGPT:

*"I am a new migrant in [your suburb]. Help me find:*
*- Free English classes*
*- A community centre*
*- Food bank or emergency food*
*- Legal help*
*- A library with free Wi-Fi"*

AI will give you names, addresses, and phone numbers. Always verify by calling.

### Community Centres
Most suburbs have a Neighbourhood House or Community Centre that offers:
- Free English conversation groups
- Computer classes
- Playgroups for children
- Social events
- Help with forms and letters

## Emergency Situations

| Situation | Number | Cost |
|-----------|--------|------|
| Police, Fire, Ambulance | **000** | Free |
| Police (non-emergency) | **131 444** | Free |
| Translating and Interpreting Service | **131 450** | Free |
| Poisons Information | **13 11 26** | Free |
| Health advice (24/7 nurse) | **1800 022 222** | Free |

**If you call 000:** Say your address first. Then say what happened. If you need an interpreter, say: *"I need an interpreter. My language is [your language]."*

---

## Using AI for Health — Safety Rules

1. **AI is not a doctor.** It can help you understand, but it cannot diagnose or treat you.
2. **For emergencies, call 000.** Do not ask ChatGPT.
3. **Always follow your doctor's advice** over what AI says.
4. **Do not type your Medicare number** or personal health details into AI.
5. AI is best for: understanding, preparing, translating, and finding services.

## Key Words

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Symptom | A sign that something is wrong (pain, fever, cough) |
| Appointment | A scheduled meeting (with a doctor, teacher, etc.) |
| Prescription | A note from a doctor for medicine |
| Mental health | Your emotional and psychological wellbeing |
| Interpreter | A person who translates spoken language in real time |
| Emergency | A dangerous situation that needs immediate help |
