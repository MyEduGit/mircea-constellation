# Chapter 5: Using AI to Practice Writing

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this chapter, you will:
- Use AI to check and improve your English writing
- Write emails, messages, and short texts with AI help
- Understand the difference between AI writing FOR you and AI helping you write

---

## Important Rule: AI Helps You Write — It Does Not Write For You

If you copy AI's words and say they are yours, that is not learning. Use AI like a teacher who checks your work and explains your mistakes.

**Good way:** You write first. Then AI checks and explains.
**Bad way:** You ask AI to write everything. You copy and paste.

---

## Method 1: Write, Then Check with ChatGPT

1. Write your text in English (even with mistakes — that's OK)
2. Paste it into ChatGPT
3. Type: *"Please correct my English. Explain each mistake so I can learn."*

**Example:**

You write: *"Yesterday I am go to shop and buy milk. The shop was close so I go other shop."*

ChatGPT corrects: *"Yesterday I went to the shop and bought milk. The shop was closed so I went to another shop."*

And explains:
- "am go" → "went" (past tense)
- "buy" → "bought" (past tense of buy)
- "close" → "closed" (adjective)
- "other shop" → "another shop" (use "another" for one different thing)

**This is better than a dictionary** because it explains YOUR specific mistakes.

---

## Method 2: Grammarly for Everyday Writing

Install Grammarly on your phone or computer. It checks your writing automatically in:
- Gmail
- WhatsApp Web
- Facebook
- Any text box in your browser

Every time you write, Grammarly shows a red underline for mistakes and suggests a fix. Over time, you start making fewer mistakes because you learn the patterns.

---

## Method 3: Email Templates

Ask ChatGPT to help you write common emails:

**Calling in sick to work:**
*"Help me write a short email to my boss. I am sick today and cannot come to work. Keep it simple and polite."*

**Asking about a bill:**
*"Help me write an email to my electricity company. I think my bill is wrong. The amount is $450 but last month it was $120. I want them to check it."*

**Replying to your child's school:**
*"The school sent me a note about a parent-teacher meeting on Friday. Help me write a reply saying I will attend."*

Save these emails as templates on your phone. Next time, change the details and send.

---

## Method 4: Daily Writing Journal

Keep a simple English journal. Write 3 sentences every day:
1. What I did today
2. How I feel
3. One new thing I learned

Once a week, paste your journal entries into ChatGPT and ask: *"Please correct my writing from this week and tell me my most common mistakes."*

## Common Writing Mistakes for ESL Students

| Mistake | Wrong | Correct |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Missing articles | "I went to shop" | "I went to **the** shop" |
| Wrong tense | "Yesterday I go" | "Yesterday I **went**" |
| Subject-verb agreement | "She have two children" | "She **has** two children" |
| Prepositions | "I arrive to work" | "I arrive **at** work" |
| Plural confusion | "I have two childs" | "I have two **children**" |

Ask ChatGPT: *"What are the most common English mistakes for [your language] speakers?"* — it will give you a personalised list.
