# Chapter 3: Staying Safe Online with AI

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this chapter, you will:
- Know the three main risks of using AI
- Understand how to protect your personal information
- Recognise AI scams and fake content

---

## Three Rules for AI Safety

### Rule 1: Never Share Personal Details with AI

AI chatbots like ChatGPT are helpful, but they are **not private**. Do not type:

- Your full name + date of birth together
- Your Tax File Number (TFN)
- Your Medicare number
- Your bank account or card numbers
- Your passwords
- Your home address

**It is OK to ask:** *"How do I apply for Medicare?"*
**It is NOT OK to type:** *"My Medicare number is 1234 5678 9, can you check my claim?"*

Think of AI like a stranger in a shop. You can ask for directions, but you would not give them your wallet.

---

### Rule 2: AI Can Be Wrong

AI is very confident, but it is **not always correct**.

AI can:
- Give you the wrong date or fact
- Make up information that sounds real (this is called a "hallucination")
- Give outdated information

**Always check important information** with an official source:
- Government websites (.gov.au)
- Your teacher
- Your doctor
- Your bank

**Example:**
You ask ChatGPT: *"What are the Centrelink payment dates for 2025?"*
ChatGPT might give you wrong dates. Always check on servicesaustralia.gov.au.

---

### Rule 3: Watch Out for AI Scams

Scammers now use AI to:
- **Write fake emails** that look real (phishing)
- **Clone voices** to pretend to be your family
- **Create fake photos** of people who don't exist
- **Make fake videos** (deepfakes)

**How to protect yourself:**
- If an email asks you to click a link urgently — stop and check
- If someone calls asking for money — hang up and call them back on the real number
- If a deal looks too good to be true — it probably is
- If you're not sure — ask your teacher, a friend, or call the Scamwatch hotline: **1300 795 995**

---

## What AI Companies Do With Your Data

When you use a free AI tool, the company may:
- Read what you type to improve their AI
- Store your conversations
- Use your data for training

This is why Rule 1 matters. If you type personal information, it could be stored.

**Tip:** Most AI tools have a "don't train on my data" setting. Ask your teacher to help you turn it on.

## Key Words

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Personal information | Details about you (name, address, numbers) |
| Scam | A trick to steal your money or information |
| Phishing | A fake email or message pretending to be real |
| Hallucination | When AI makes up wrong information |
| Deepfake | A fake video or photo made by AI |
| Privacy | Keeping your information safe and private |
