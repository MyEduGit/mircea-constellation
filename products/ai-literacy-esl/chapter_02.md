# Chapter 2: AI Tools You Can Use Today

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this chapter, you will:
- Know five free AI tools that help with English
- Be able to open and use each tool
- Understand what each tool is best for

---

## Five Free AI Tools

These tools are **free** and work on your phone or computer.

### 1. ChatGPT (by OpenAI)
**What it does:** You type a question in any language. It answers in English (or any language you choose).

**Best for:** Practising English, asking questions, getting explanations

**How to use it:**
1. Go to chatgpt.com on your phone or computer
2. Create a free account (email + password)
3. Type your question in the box
4. Press Enter

**Try this now:**
Type: *"Explain what Medicare is in Australia. Use simple English."*

---

### 2. Google Translate
**What it does:** Translates words, sentences, or whole pages between languages.

**Best for:** Understanding documents, signs, letters from school

**How to use it:**
1. Open the Google Translate app (free on iPhone and Android)
2. Choose your language on the left
3. Choose English on the right
4. Type, speak, or take a photo

**Special feature:** Point your phone camera at a sign or letter. Google Translate shows the English on your screen instantly.

---

### 3. Grammarly
**What it does:** Checks your English writing for mistakes and suggests corrections.

**Best for:** Emails, job applications, assignments

**How to use it:**
1. Go to grammarly.com
2. Create a free account
3. Paste your writing
4. Grammarly highlights mistakes in red and suggests fixes

**Try this now:**
Type this sentence with mistakes: *"I am go to the shops yesterday."*
Watch Grammarly fix it to: *"I went to the shops yesterday."*

---

### 4. Elsa Speak
**What it does:** Listens to you speak English and tells you how to improve your pronunciation.

**Best for:** Speaking practice, pronunciation, confidence

**How to use it:**
1. Download ELSA Speak from the App Store or Google Play (free version available)
2. Choose your first language
3. Follow the speaking exercises
4. The app shows you which sounds to improve

---

### 5. Microsoft Copilot
**What it does:** Like ChatGPT, but built into Microsoft products. Helps with writing, summarising, and answering questions.

**Best for:** Writing emails, understanding long documents, homework help

**How to use it:**
1. Go to copilot.microsoft.com
2. Sign in with a Microsoft account (free)
3. Type your question

---

## Which Tool Should I Use?

| I want to... | Use this tool |
|-------------|--------------|
| Ask a question in my language | ChatGPT or Google Translate |
| Check my English writing | Grammarly |
| Practise speaking English | ELSA Speak |
| Understand a letter or document | Google Translate (camera) |
| Write an email | Grammarly + ChatGPT |
| Prepare for a job interview | ChatGPT |

## Practice Activities

### Activity 1: Set Up One Tool (Cert I)
Choose ONE tool from this chapter. Set it up on your phone. Show your teacher that it works.

Tool I chose: _______________
It works: YES / NO

### Activity 2: Compare Two Tools (Cert II)
Use ChatGPT AND Google Translate to translate the same sentence from your language to English. Write both translations below. Are they the same or different?

My sentence: _______________
ChatGPT translation: _______________
Google Translate translation: _______________
Same or different? _______________

### Activity 3: Write a Review (Cert III)
Choose one AI tool from this chapter. Use it for one week. Then write a short review (5-8 sentences):
- What is the tool?
- What did you use it for?
- What was good about it?
- What was not good?
- Would you recommend it? Why?

## Teacher's Note
This is a hands-on chapter. Students should have phones ready. Walk around the room and help with account creation — many students struggle with email/password steps. Pair stronger students with beginners.

The ChatGPT exercise works best if students ask questions about their real life (Medicare, Centrelink, school enrolment).

Estimated class time: 90 minutes (allow time for setup)
Materials needed: students' phones, Wi-Fi access
