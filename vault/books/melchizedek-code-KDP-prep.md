# The Melchizedek Code: Amazon KDP Publishing Prep

Your highest income leverage. This is the step-by-step to get the book live
on Amazon. Nothing here needs code, it's a checklist you (and I) work
through together.

## Stage 1: Amazon accounts (you, ~20 min)
- [ ] Create a **KDP account** at kdp.amazon.com (use your Amazon login)
- [ ] Complete **tax interview** (Australian, you'll need TFN/details;
      the US-Australia treaty means 0% or low withholding)
- [ ] Add **bank account** for royalty payments (AUD)
- [ ] Set up **Amazon Author Central** (author.amazon.com) for the
      "Mircea G. Matthews" author page

## Stage 2: Manuscript formatting (I help)
- [ ] Front matter: title page, copyright, dedication, contents
- [ ] Consistent chapter headings, scene breaks
- [ ] Export to KDP-ready format: **.docx** or **reflowable EPUB**
- [ ] For print (paperback): set trim size (6×9" is standard), margins,
      page numbers, embedded fonts

## Stage 3: Cover
- [ ] **eBook cover**: 1600×2560px, under 50MB, JPG/TIFF
- [ ] **Paperback cover**: full wrap (front+spine+back) sized to page count
- Options: KDP's free Cover Creator, a Canva template, or a Fiverr/99designs
  designer (~$50–300 for professional). I can draft cover copy + a design
  brief either way.

## Stage 4: Metadata (this is where sales are won: I draft all of it)
- [ ] **Title + subtitle**: keyword-rich, clear promise
- [ ] **Book description**: the most important sales text; I'll write it
- [ ] **7 keywords**: researched for what readers actually search
- [ ] **2 categories**: pick low-competition ones to hit bestseller flags
- [ ] **Author bio**: your story as a Urantia student in Melbourne

## Stage 5: Pricing & royalties
- [ ] eBook: **$2.99–$9.99** → qualifies for **70% royalty** (vs 35% outside
      that band). $4.99–$6.99 is a common sweet spot for non-fiction.
- [ ] Paperback: priced to cover Amazon's print cost + your margin
- [ ] Enrol in **KDP Select**? (90-day Amazon exclusivity → Kindle Unlimited
      reads + promo tools. Worth it for a first book to build visibility.)

## Stage 6: Launch
- [ ] Set a **pre-order** (builds rank before day one)
- [ ] Announce to your Substack list (tie-in to A Third Story)
- [ ] First-week push: ask early readers for **honest reviews** (reviews =
 visibility; never buy them: Amazon bans accounts for it)
- [ ] Link the book from your Substack About page and every post footer

- The newsletter builds the **audience**; the book **monetises** it.
- Each Substack post can end (occasionally) with: "If you'd like the fuller

- Down the line: collect the best posts into a **second book** (low effort,
 high value: the writing already exists in `vault/substack/`).
