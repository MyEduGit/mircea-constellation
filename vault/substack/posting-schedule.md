# Posting Schedule: A Third Story (LOCKED)

This is the standing rhythm. Do not re-decide it weekly, just follow it.
Substack's native scheduler does the actual publishing; you set the slot once
per post and it fires automatically.

## Frequency: 3 posts per week

Daily is too much for a new newsletter (inbox fatigue → unsubscribes).
Three steady, never-missed posts beats seven ignored ones.

## Send slot: set Substack timezone to **US Eastern** (set once, inherited by all posts)

| Day | Send time (ET) | Lands as | Why |
|-----|----------------|----------|-----|
| **Tuesday** | 10:30am ET | 9:30am CT / 7:30am PT | Mid-morning open window, all 4 US zones awake |
| **Thursday** | 10:30am ET | 9:30am CT / 7:30am PT | Second-best weekday; carries into weekend |
| **Sunday** | 9:00am ET | 8:00am CT / 6:00am PT | Reflective content over-performs Sunday mornings |

**Why 10:30am ET (not pure East coast):** US audience is ~47% Eastern,
~29% Central, ~17% Pacific. 8am ET would hit the West at 5am (asleep).
10:30am ET is the only slot that catches East, Central, and Pacific all
inside the 8–11am local "good" window. Australian readers get it as an
evening read: a bonus, not the target.

## Workflow

1. Claude writes posts ahead → banked as numbered files in `vault/substack/`.
2. Mircea verifies (doctrine check: catch any wrong paper number or claim).
3. Mircea pastes into Substack, sets the scheduled slot above.
4. Substack auto-publishes and emails. No one stays up to hit send.

## Sharing rhythm

- Publish day: post goes out via email automatically.
- Next morning: share the post link into one community (ANZURA, Fellowship
 newcomers' board): rotate, don't spam the same place.
