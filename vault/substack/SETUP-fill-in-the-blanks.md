# Substack Setup — Fill-in-the-blanks sheet

Work top to bottom. Each box below = one field in Substack. Copy the text,
paste it in, move to the next. Tick the checkbox when done.

Everything you need is on THIS page — you don't have to open any other file.

---

## ☐ STEP 1 — "Set up the basics" (Getting started checklist)

Click **Set up the basics**, then fill these:

**Publication name:**
```
M. G. Matthews
```

**Short description / Introduction (one line):**
```
Short, honest letters about the teachings of The Urantia Book — for anyone who's found neither science nor religion fully satisfying.
```

**Profile / author photo:** upload a simple photo of yourself or a calm image
(a sunrise, a sky). A real face builds more trust than a logo. Skip if you'd
rather — not a blocker.

---

## ☐ STEP 2 — Settings → Introduction (the longer "About")

Paste this into the Introduction / About box:

```
Most of us were handed two stories about where we came from. One from science, one from religion. For a long time I felt both left something out — neither quite explained who God is, or why we're here, in a way that satisfied me.

Then I came across a book that offered a third picture: vast, strange, oddly coherent. It's called The Urantia Book, and I've spent much of my life reading it.

This newsletter is where I write about what I've found there — in short, plain pieces, a few times a week. One idea at a time. No jargon I don't explain. No attempt to convert you to anything. The full text of the book is free at urantia.org; I'm just someone offering a way in.

I write as M. G. Matthews. I'm in my seventies, based in Melbourne, Australia — not a priest or scholar, just a long-time reader who believes these ideas matter, especially now.

If you've ever wanted a bigger answer than the ones on offer, subscribe (it's free) and the next letter will land in your inbox.

These letters are my own reading and reflection — not official teaching from any Urantia organisation.
```

## ☐ STEP 4 — Settings → require email confirmation: turn ON

Find **"Require email address confirmation"** and switch it ON.
Keeps bots and dead addresses off your list so your open-rate stays honest.

---

## ☐ STEP 5 — Get your share link

Click **"Share your Substack"** (or open your published post and copy the URL
from the browser bar). It looks like:
```
mirceamatthews.substack.com/p/a-third-story-about-where-we-came-from
```
Use this link to:
- fill the [LINK] in the welcome email (Step 3)
- fill the [LINK] in your community share messages (Step 6)

---

## ☐ STEP 6 — Delete the tutorial draft

In **Drafts**, the item "How to use the Substack editor" is Substack's own
tutorial stub, not yours. Open it → ⋯ menu → **Delete**. Keeps your workspace
clean.

---

---

## DELIBERATELY SKIPPED — "Turn on paid subscriptions"

Leave this OFF. You have no subscribers yet; a price tag in front of strangers
only adds friction and a Stripe chore. Free-only is correct at launch.
Revisit ONLY once you have a few hundred engaged free readers who'd genuinely
pay. Don't let the checklist guilt you into it.
