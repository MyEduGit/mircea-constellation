## LONG (the About page itself)

### A Third Story

Most of us were handed two stories about where we came from. One from
science, one from religion. For a long time I felt both left something out ,
neither quite explained who God is, or why we're here, in a way that
satisfied me.

Then I came across a book that offered a third picture: vast, strange, oddly
coherent. It's called The Urantia Book, and I've spent much of my life
reading it.

This newsletter is where I write about what I've found there, in short,
plain pieces, a few times a week. One idea at a time. No jargon I don't
explain. No attempt to convert you to anything.

**What you'll get here:**
- Brief letters (a two-minute read) on the ideas that have stayed with me ,
  what the book says about God, about the meaning of suffering, about what
  happens when we die, about the man who walked through Galilee, and about
  where it all might be heading.
- An honest voice. I'll tell you when the book's science is shaky, when an
  idea is hard, and when I simply don't know. I'd rather earn your trust
  than win an argument.
- An open door, never a hard sell. The full text of the book is free to
  read at urantia.org. I'm just someone offering a way in.

**Who I am:** I write as M. G. Matthews. I'm in my seventies, based in
Melbourne, Australia, and I'm not a priest, scholar, or official anything ,
just a long-time reader who believes these ideas matter, especially now.

**Why subscribe:** If you've ever wanted a bigger answer than the ones on
offer, subscribe (it's free) and the next letter will land in your inbox.
That's the whole offer. I'm glad you're here.

*These letters are my own reading and reflection, not official teaching
from any Urantia organisation.*
