# The Place We Go Next

I've written before about what the Urantia Book says happens when we die, that physical death is not the end, that survival is real, that the person you are continues on. But I haven't said much about *where*.

The book calls them the mansion worlds.

The phrase comes from the gospel of John, "In my Father's house are many mansions", and the Urantia Book takes it quite literally. When you die, the book says, you awaken on one of seven worlds orbiting a system headquarters called Jerusem. Which one you wake up on depends, roughly, on where you were in your development when you left this world. But wherever you begin, the direction is always the same: forward.

These worlds are places of learning, growth, and repair. Whatever was left undone in this life, whatever wounds were not healed, whatever capacities were not developed, whatever relationships were broken, there is time and opportunity to address it. The book describes schools, teachers, meaningful work, reunion with people who have gone before. It is not a static heaven of clouds and harps. It is a continuation of the same project you began here: the long, soul-building journey toward God.

One detail has always stayed with me. On the mansion worlds, the book says, you will have a *morontia body*, neither purely physical like this one, nor purely spiritual, but something in between, more real in some ways than what you now inhabit. You will be recognisably yourself: your personality, your memories, your particular way of loving and wondering and struggling. Death does not erase you. It moves you on.

I am 70 years old. I think about this more than I once did, not with fear but with something closer to curiosity. The people I have loved who have gone ahead of me, I find I believe, genuinely, that they are not lost. They are on the next stage of a journey that has no end.

The book's account of survival is one of the most detailed and coherent I've encountered. I'm not asking you to take it on faith. I'm asking whether a universe as vast and purposeful as the one the book describes would really end a person at physical death, and whether your own deepest instinct agrees.

The full text is free at [urantia.org](https://www.urantia.org). Papers 47–48 describe the mansion worlds in detail.
