# Reader Growth Kit: A Third Story

You have great writing and 6 readers. The whole game now is **distribution**.
This kit is everything you need to grow, copy-paste ready. Pick one action a
day; don't try to do them all at once.

---

## The one principle
Good writing doesn't spread by itself. Every new reader comes from either
(a) you putting the post in front of a relevant community, or (b) an existing
reader sharing it. Your job for the next 90 days is simply to **show up and
share, consistently.** Numbers follow.

A realistic, encouraging target: **100 free subscribers by end of August.**
From 6, that sounds huge. It isn't, it's roughly one new reader a day. Very
doable with steady sharing.

## Channel 2: The wider "spiritually curious" audience

These people have never heard of the Urantia Book. They find you through the
*questions* you raise, not the book itself. Lead with the human question, not
the source.

- **Substack Notes** (the built-in feed, post 3–5×/week, it's the #1 growth
  engine on Substack now)
- **Reddit**: r/spirituality, r/religion, r/awakened, r/NDE, share the
  *idea*, link in a comment, never spam
- **Quora**: answer questions like "Is the universe friendly?", "What happens
  when we die?" with a short answer + link
- **Facebook / Instagram**: your own profile, one post per newsletter
- **X / Twitter**: short excerpt threads (see Channel 4)

---

## Channel 3: Substack's own engine (do this weekly)

Substack rewards activity inside its ecosystem. Costs nothing, compounds fast.

1. **Recommendations**: find 5–10 Substacks on faith, spirituality,
   philosophy, meaning. Subscribe, then "recommend" them from your dashboard.
   Many will recommend you back → free cross-promotion.
2. **Notes**: post a short thought or a 2-line excerpt from each new piece.
   This is where new readers discover you now.
3. **Reply to comments** (Jarvis + I help with this), engaged comment
   sections rank higher and feel alive to newcomers.
4. **Engage on other newsletters**: leave thoughtful comments on bigger
   spirituality Substacks. Your name links back to yours.

## Channel 5: Ask your readers to share (the multiplier)

At the bottom of every 4th or 5th post, add one warm line:

> If this resonated, the kindest thing you could do is forward it to one
> person who might need it. That's how small newsletters like this one grow ,
> one trusted hand to another. Thank you.

People share when you ask, gently, and almost never when you don't.

## What NOT to do
- Don't buy followers or use engagement bots, kills credibility.
- Don't spam the same link into 20 groups in one day, you'll get banned.
- Don't turn on paid yet, grow free to ~1,000 first (see earlier advice).
- Don't check stats obsessively. Share, then look away. Growth is slow then
  sudden.

---

## When to revisit paid subscriptions
When you hit **~1,000 free subscribers**, have published consistently for
**3+ months**, and a few readers have asked or pledged. Not before. Until
then: free, friction-free, focused entirely on reach.
