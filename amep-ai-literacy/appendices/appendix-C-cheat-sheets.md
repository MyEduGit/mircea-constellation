## Cheat Sheet C-1: Claude Chat (claude.ai)

**How to open Claude:**
1. Open Chrome or Safari.
2. Type **claude.ai** in the address bar.
3. Press Enter / Go.
4. Find the message box at the bottom.
5. Type your question. Press the arrow (→).

**Useful questions to ask:**
- "Please check my sentence: [your sentence]"
- "What does the word [word] mean? Give an example."
- "Please explain this in simple English: [text]"
- "Please help me write an email to [place] about [topic]."
- "Can you give me three examples of [grammar rule]?"

**Remember:**
- Do NOT type your real name, address, or ID number.
- Claude can be wrong. Always check important information.
- You do not need to pay. Claude is free to use.

⏱ *Free tier features may change. Check claude.ai.*

## Cheat Sheet C-2: Voice Mode

**How to use your voice in Claude:**

**Method 1 — Phone keyboard microphone:**
1. Open Claude on your phone.
2. Tap inside the message box.
3. Look for the microphone icon (🎤) on your keyboard.
4. Tap it. Speak your message.
5. Check the words. Then tap the send arrow.

**Method 2 — Claude app voice mode:**
1. Open the Claude app on your phone.
2. Tap the microphone icon inside the app.
3. Speak. Wait. Read or listen to the reply.

**Useful voice prompts:**
- "How do I pronounce the word [word]?"
- "Hello. Can you help me practise English?"
- "What does [word] mean?"

**Remember:**
- Do NOT say your address, passport number, or bank details.
- If the phone misunderstands you, type the word instead.

## Cheat Sheet C-3: Claude for Chrome

**How to set up:**
1. Open **Google Chrome** on a laptop or Android phone.
2. Go to: **chromewebstore.google.com**
3. Search: **Claude for Chrome** (by Anthropic)
4. Click **Add to Chrome**. Click **Add extension**.
5. Sign in to your Anthropic account.
6. Look for the Claude icon (🔶) in the top-right corner of Chrome.

**How to use on a webpage:**
1. Open the webpage you want to understand.
2. Click the Claude icon (🔶) — a panel opens on the right.
3. Highlight the text you find hard.
4. In the panel, type: "Please explain this in simple English."
5. Press Enter. Read Claude's explanation.

**Remember:**
- Close the Claude panel when you are on a banking or medical site.
- Claude can see only the page you have open — not your other tabs.
- iPhone users: Chrome extensions do not work on iPhones. ⏱

## Cheat Sheet C-4: Sharing Documents with Claude

**Three ways — choose the safest one for your document:**

**Method 1 — Type the question (safest):**
Type just the question from the form into Claude.
Do NOT include your name or personal details.

**Method 2 — Copy and paste text:**
1. Select the text in your document.
2. Press Ctrl+C (laptop) or long-press → Copy (phone).
3. Go to Claude. Tap the message box.
4. Press Ctrl+V (laptop) or long-press → Paste (phone).
5. Delete any personal information from the pasted text.
6. Ask your question.

**Method 3 — Upload a file:**
1. Click the paperclip icon (📎) in Claude.
2. Choose your file.
3. ⚠️ Remove personal information from the file BEFORE uploading.

**Before you share anything — ask yourself:**
"Would I be comfortable if a stranger could read this?"
If no → use Method 1 (type the question only).

## Cheat Sheet C-5: Claude Code (IT-stream only)

**What is Claude Code?**
Claude Code is a version of Claude that works in the computer terminal.
It helps people who write computer programs.

**When to use it:**
- When you have an error in your code and cannot find the problem.
- When you want to understand what a piece of code does.
- When you want to write a new small program.

**How to start (terminal):**
```
claude
```
*(Requires Node.js installed and Claude Code set up — see docs.anthropic.com/claude-code ⏱)*

**Useful Claude Code prompts:**
- "What is wrong with this code: [paste code]"
- "Explain what this code does."
- "Fix this error: [error message]"
- "Write a simple Python program that [describes what you want]"

**Remember:**
- Do NOT paste code that contains passwords or private data.
- Always test the code Claude writes before using it.
- Claude Code requires a paid account. ⏱

## Cheat Sheet C-6: Privacy Quick Reference (For Students)

**NEVER type or say these in AI:**

| ❌ Not safe | ✅ Safe alternative |
|------------|-------------------|
| Your full name | A made-up name: "Anna" |
| Your home address | "[my address]" or a made-up one |
| Your phone number | Leave it out |
| Your passport number | Leave it out |
| Your visa number | Leave it out |
| Your bank details | Leave it out |
| Your children's names | "my child" |
| Your medical history | Describe the situation generally |

**Australian Privacy Rights:**
If an AI system made an unfair decision about you, contact:
- **OAIC:** oaic.gov.au ⏱
- A community legal centre near you.
