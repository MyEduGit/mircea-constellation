## S-8.5 · Do It With Me

### How to share a document with Claude:

**Method 1 — Type the question (safest):**
1. Open Claude on your device.
2. Type the question or section of the form you do not understand.
3. Ask: "Please explain this question and tell me what to write."

**Method 2 — Copy and paste text:**
1. Open your document.
2. Select the text you want to explain. Press Ctrl+C (or long-press on phone).
3. Go to Claude. Tap the message box.
4. Press Ctrl+V to paste (or long-press and choose Paste).
5. Type your question before the pasted text.
   Example: "Please explain this text: [pasted text]"

**Method 3 — Upload a file (Claude.ai):**
1. Open claude.ai.
2. Look for the paperclip icon (📎) in the message box.
3. Tap or click it.
4. Choose your file from your device.
5. Ask your question.

> ⚠️ **Before you upload:** Remove personal information from the document first,
> or use Method 1 or 2 instead.

## S-8.9 · Safety Box

🔒 **Before you share a file with Claude:**

**Ask yourself these questions:**

| Question | Safe? |
|----------|-------|
| Does this file have my full name and address? | Remove them first |
| Does this file have my passport or visa number? | Do NOT upload |
| Does this file have my bank account details? | Do NOT upload |
| Does this file have my children's names or school details? | Do NOT upload |
| Does this file have a form question that I do not understand? | Safe to type the question |

> **Consent checkpoint:**
> Every time you upload a file to an AI program,
> you are giving that program permission to read it.
> Only upload what you are comfortable sharing.

**AI can be wrong. How do we check?**

> Claude can explain what a form question means.
> But Claude cannot fill in the form correctly for you.
> You know your own information — Claude does not.
> Always read what Claude writes before you put it on an official form.
> Ask your teacher or a settlement worker if you are not sure.
