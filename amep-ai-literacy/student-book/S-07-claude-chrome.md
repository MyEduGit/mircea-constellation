## S-7.5 · Do It With Me

👀 **Watch. Then do together.**

🌐 *(You need the internet and Google Chrome on a laptop or Android phone.)*

### Setting up Claude in Chrome:

1. Open Google Chrome on your laptop.
2. Go to the Chrome Web Store.
   Type in the address bar: **chromewebstore.google.com**
3. In the search box, type: **Claude for Chrome** or **Claude AI**
4. Find the official Claude extension by Anthropic.
5. Click **Add to Chrome**.
6. A box appears. Read it. Click **Add extension**.
7. You will see the Claude icon (a small orange/gold icon) in the top right of Chrome.

### Using Claude in Chrome:

1. Open any webpage you want to understand.
   (Try a government health or transport page.)
2. Click the Claude icon in the top right corner.
3. A panel opens on the right side of your screen.
4. Highlight (click and drag) over text you want explained.
5. In the Claude panel, type:
   **"Please explain this text in simple English."**
6. Press Enter.
7. Read Claude's explanation in the panel.

## S-7.9 · Safety Box

🔒 **What Claude in Chrome can see:**

| Claude CAN see | Claude CANNOT see |
|---------------|------------------|
| Text on the webpage you have open | Your other browser tabs |
| Text you highlight | Your emails |
| Your questions in the panel | Your files on your computer |
| | Other apps on your phone |

> **Rule:** Only open Claude in Chrome when you are reading a webpage.
> Close the Claude panel when you are doing something private.
> Do not use Claude in Chrome when you are on a banking website.

**AI can be wrong. How do we check?**

> Claude's summary of a webpage may leave out important details.
> Always scroll back to the original page.
> For health, legal, or financial information — read the full official text.
> Or ask a qualified professional to explain it to you.
