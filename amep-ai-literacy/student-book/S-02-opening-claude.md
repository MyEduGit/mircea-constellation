## S-2.5 · Do It With Me

👀 **Watch. Then do together.**

🌐 *(You need the internet for this activity.)*

### On a phone:

1. Open your browser app. (Chrome or Safari.)
2. Tap the address bar at the top.
3. Type: **claude.ai**
4. Tap **Go** or press the Enter key.
5. Wait for the page to load.
6. Look for a box at the bottom of the page. It says: "Message Claude…"
7. Tap inside that box.
8. Type: **Hello. What can you help me with?**
9. Tap the arrow button (→) to send.
10. Read the reply.

### On a laptop:

1. Open Chrome or another browser.
2. Click the address bar at the top.
3. Type: **claude.ai** and press Enter.
4. Look for the box at the bottom. It says: "Message Claude…"
5. Click inside that box.
6. Type: **Hello. What can you help me with?**
7. Press the Enter key to send.
8. Read the reply.

---

**After you try, answer these questions:**

- How many sentences did Claude write?
- Did you understand any words in the reply?
- What word did you NOT understand?

## S-2.9 · Safety Box

🔒 **Remember these rules every time you open Claude.**

**Do NOT type:**
- Your real name
- Your address or suburb
- Your phone number
- Your passport or visa number
- Your bank account number
- Your doctor's information

**It is fine to type:**
- "Hello"
- "What does this word mean?"
- "Can you check my sentence?"
- "How do I write an email?"

> **Tip:** Use a made-up name in your examples.
> Instead of "My name is Maria Gonzalez," write "My name is Anna."
