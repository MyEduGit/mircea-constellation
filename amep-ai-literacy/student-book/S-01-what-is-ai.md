## S-1.4 · Look and Listen

🔊 **Listen to your teacher read this. Follow with your eyes.**

---

**Maria and her teacher, Sam:**

> **Sam:** Maria, have you heard the word "AI"?
>
> **Maria:** I hear it on TV. But I do not know what it is.
>
> **Sam:** AI is a computer program. You can type a question.
> The program reads your question. Then it writes an answer.
>
> **Maria:** Like sending a message to a person?
>
> **Sam:** Yes. But there is no person. It is a program.
>
> **Maria:** Can it make mistakes?
>
> **Sam:** Yes. AI can make mistakes. We always check the answer.
>
> **Maria:** OK. I want to try.
>
> **Sam:** Good. Let us start.

---

**Read again. Answer yes or no.**

1. Maria knows what AI is. **Yes / No**
2. AI is a program. **Yes / No**
3. AI never makes mistakes. **Yes / No**

*(Answers: 1. No · 2. Yes · 3. No)*

## S-1.5 · Do It With Me

👀 **Watch your teacher. Then do it together.**

Your teacher will show you a phone or a computer.
You will see an AI program called **Claude**.

**Steps:**

1. Look at your teacher's screen.
2. You see a white box at the bottom of the screen.
3. That is where you type your question.
4. Watch your teacher type: **Hello. What is AI?**
5. Watch what happens next.
6. The program writes an answer.
7. Read the answer together.

---

**After you watch, answer these questions with your teacher:**

- What colour is the chat box?
- How long did the answer take?
- Did the answer look long or short?

## S-1.9 · Safety Box

🔒 **Read this carefully. It is important.**

**When you use AI, do NOT type:**

- Your full name
- Your address
- Your phone number
- Your passport number or visa number
- Your bank details
- Your children's names
- Your medical history
- Photos of your ID card

> **Why?**
> AI programs are made by companies.
> Companies store what you type.
> Keep your personal information safe.

**It is safe to type:**
- A question about English
- A question about the weather
- A question about how something works
