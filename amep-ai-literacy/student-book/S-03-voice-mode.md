## S-3.5 · Do It With Me

👀 **Watch. Then do together.**

🌐 *(You need the internet and the Claude app or website.)*

### Using voice on a phone (Claude app):

1. Open the Claude app on your phone.
2. Look for a microphone icon. It is usually at the right side of the message box.
3. Tap the microphone icon.
4. Speak clearly: **"Hello. Can you help me with an English word?"**
5. Stop speaking. The phone will listen for a moment.
6. Your words will appear in the message box.
7. Tap the send arrow (→) to send.
8. Read Claude's reply. Or tap the speaker icon to hear it read aloud.

### Using voice input on any phone (voice keyboard):

1. Open claude.ai in your browser.
2. Tap inside the message box.
3. On your keyboard, look for a microphone icon. Tap it.
4. Speak your message.
5. Your words appear in the box.
6. Tap the send arrow to send.

---

**After you try, answer these questions:**

- Did the phone understand your words?
- Did any words come out wrong?
- What word did you want to say?

## S-3.9 · Safety Box

🔒 **Safety reminder for voice mode.**

When you speak to AI:
- Do not say your address or phone number.
- Do not say your visa number or passport number.
- Do not say your bank details.

> **Tip:** Your voice is recorded when you use voice mode.
> Only practise English words and sentences.
> Do not have a private conversation near AI.
