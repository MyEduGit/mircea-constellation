## S-6.5 · Do It With Me

### Two types of Claude questions:

| Type | Example | Does Claude search? |
|------|---------|-------------------|
| General knowledge | "What is a democracy?" | Sometimes — uses training |
| Current information | "What time does the clinic open today?" | Yes — uses web search ⏱ |

> **Important:** Claude's web search is not always enabled.
> ⏱ Check with your teacher if Claude has web search turned on in your version.

---

### Useful prompts for finding information:

**Bus or train times:**
> "What buses run from [suburb] to [place] on weekday mornings?"

**Opening hours:**
> "What are the opening hours of [place name] in [suburb]?"

**Today's news (simple):**
> "What is one piece of news in Australia today? Please use simple English."

**Checking a fact:**
> "Is it true that [fact]? What is the source?"

---

### Try it now:

1. Open Claude on your device.
2. Ask Claude one question about current information.
   Choose from:
   - Bus or train times near you.
   - Opening hours of a nearby government office.
   - Today's weather forecast.
3. Read the reply.
4. Look for the source. Claude may give a website name.
5. Open that website yourself to check.

## S-6.9 · Safety Box

🔒 **When you search for information:**

- Do not tell Claude your exact location or home address.
- Say your suburb name only if you need local information.
- If Claude gives you a website, check the website yourself — do not give Claude your login details for that website.
- Government websites in Australia usually end in **.gov.au** — these are official and safe.
