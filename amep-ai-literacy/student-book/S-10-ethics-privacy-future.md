## S-10.6 · Do It With Me

### What is bias in AI?

AI learns from human-written text.
Humans sometimes have unfair ideas about groups of people.
AI can learn those unfair ideas.

**Example:**
If an AI was trained on old job advertisements,
it might suggest men more often than women for some jobs.
That is bias. It is not fair.

**What can you do?**
- Notice if an AI answer seems unfair.
- Report it using the feedback button (a thumbs-down icon).
- Ask your teacher or a trusted person.

## S-10.10 · Safety Box

🔒 **Your rights with AI:**

You have the right to:
- Say no to AI in situations where you do not want it.
- Ask a company how they use your information.
- Ask for a human to review an AI decision that affects you.
- Report an AI system that seems unfair or wrong.

> **In Australia:**
> The Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC)
> protects your privacy rights. Website: **oaic.gov.au** ⏱
