## S-5.4 · Look and Listen

🔊 **Listen. Follow with your eyes.**

---

**Fatima received a letter from her electricity company. She does not understand it. She asks Claude.**

> **Fatima types:** I have a letter from my electricity company. It says:
> "Your account has an outstanding balance of $142.00. If payment is not received by 15 June, your supply may be interrupted."
> What does this mean? Please use simple words.
>
> **Claude replies:** This letter means you owe the electricity company $142.00.
> You need to pay this amount before 15 June.
> If you do not pay by that date, they may turn off your electricity.
> To pay, look for a phone number or website on the letter.
> You can call them or pay online.
>
> **Fatima:** Thank you. Can you help me write an email to ask for more time to pay?
>
> **Claude:** Of course. Here is a draft email for you:
>
> Subject: Request for payment extension — Account [your account number]
>
> Dear [Company Name] Customer Service,
>
> I am writing about my account. I received a notice about an outstanding balance of $142.00.
> I would like to request more time to make this payment.
> Could you please let me know if an extension is possible?
>
> Thank you for your help.
> Kind regards,
> [Your name]

## S-5.5 · Do It With Me

### Useful prompts for real-life situations:

**To understand a letter or document:**
> "Please explain this letter in simple words: [paste the text]"

**To write a formal email:**
> "Please help me write a formal email to [a shop / a doctor / my landlord].
> I want to say: [your message in simple words]"

**To make an email more polite:**
> "Please make this email more polite: [your email]"

**To understand a word in a document:**
> "What does '[word]' mean in this sentence: [paste the sentence]"

## S-5.9 · Safety Box

🔒 **When asking Claude to help with letters or emails:**

- Replace your real name with a made-up name.
- Replace your address with a made-up address or write "[my address]".
- Replace your account number with "[account number]".
- Never paste your full passport, visa, or medical documents into AI.

> **Why?** Even if Claude seems private, the text you type may be stored.
> Always remove your real personal details before pasting.

**AI can be wrong. How do we check?**

> Before you send any email that Claude helped you write:
> 1. Read it yourself. Does it sound right?
> 2. Ask your teacher or a trusted friend to read it.
> 3. Check the key facts. Did Claude use the correct date and amount?
> 4. Make sure your real details (name, account number) are in the final version.
