### Design Principles

**Safety first.** Every module includes a Safety Box. Students learn from day one
not to share personal information with AI.

**Paper always available.** Every module has a complete offline alternative.
No student is excluded because they lack a device or data.

**Teacher confidence, not teacher expertise.** You do not need to know how AI
works — the Teacher's Book tells you exactly what to say and what to do
when things go wrong.

**Dignity at every level.** Written for adults. No cartoon robots. No
condescension. CEFR Pre-A1 materials use adult contexts and adult respect.
