## How to Use This Book

### For Students

This book helps you learn English.
It also helps you use AI tools.
AI tools are computer programs that can talk with you.

You will learn step by step.
You do not need to know anything about AI before you start.
You do not need to be good with computers before you start.

Every lesson has:
- New words to learn.
- Something to listen to.
- Something to do.
- Something to write.
- Something to talk about.

### For Teachers

This is a two-book set.

**Book A — Student Book** is for students.
**Book B — Teacher's Book** is for you.

Every Student Book page has a matching Teacher's Book page.
Student pages are numbered **S-1.1, S-1.2** and so on.
Teacher pages are numbered **T-1.1, T-1.2** and so on.

You do not need to be an AI expert to teach these lessons.
The Teacher's Book tells you exactly what to say and do.
It also tells you what can go wrong and how to fix it.

## Safety Charter

### What we agree in this class:

1. We do not share other students' personal information.
2. We do not share our own personal information with AI.
3. We ask questions freely. There are no silly questions.
4. We help each other. We do not laugh at mistakes.
5. We check AI answers. AI can be wrong.
6. We tell the teacher if something on a screen makes us uncomfortable.

**Personal information means:**
Your full name · Your address · Your phone number
Your passport number · Your visa number · Your bank details
Your children's names · Your medical history · Photos of your ID
