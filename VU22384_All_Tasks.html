Student Name
Date
Learning Outcomes
  • Practise speaking about goals using new vocabulary.
  • Interview different classmates.
  • Write clear and strong goals.
  • Speak with one new student.

Section 1 — Key Vocabulary

Word / Phrase Meaning Example Sentence
goal something you want to achieve My main goal is to improve my English.
short-term goal a goal for the next few weeks or months A short-term goal is to speak English every day.
long-term goal a goal for 1 year or more My long-term goal is to get a job in Australia.
achieve to successfully reach a goal I want to achieve my goal of passing this course.
aspiration a strong hope or ambition Her aspiration is to become a nurse.
specific clear and exact, not general Make your goals specific so you know what to do.
motivation the reason you want to do something My motivation is my family.
challenge something difficult you have to deal with A challenge for me is understanding fast speakers.
progress improvement over time I can see my progress in my writing.

Section 1 — What Is a Portfolio?

A portfolio is a collection of your work that shows your learning and progress. It can include writing samples, worksheets, recordings, certificates, and reflections.
